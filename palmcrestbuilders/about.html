About Palm Crest Builders

Licensed Southern California General Building Contractor

Experienced builders serving LA County — honest pricing, quality craftsmanship, and one accountable team from design to done.

Palm Crest Builders is built on a simple idea: homeowners and business owners across Los Angeles County deserve a general contractor who shows up on time, explains the work clearly, and stands behind every detail. We're a licensed California Class B general building contractor focused on high-quality remodeling and construction across LA County and Southern California.

Our crews handle everything from kitchen and bathroom remodels to room additions, ADUs, garage conversions, foundations, and ground-up new construction. As a design-build contractor, we manage architecture, engineering, permits, structural work, mechanicals, and finishes under one contract — so you have a single point of accountability from the first sketch to the final walk-through.

We serve homeowners from Santa Clarita to Long Beach, from the Westside to the San Gabriel Valley — and everywhere in between. Whatever the neighborhood, the commitment is the same: quality materials, skilled builders, code-compliant construction, and a workmanship warranty you can count on.
