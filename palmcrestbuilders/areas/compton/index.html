Compton Contractor FAQs

Do you pull building permits in Compton?

Yes — we handle plans, plan check, and permits through the Compton building and safety department for every remodel, addition, and ADU, and we schedule all required inspections.

Can I build an ADU in Compton?

In most cases, yes. Under California and Compton rules, most single-family lots qualify for a detached, attached, or garage-conversion ADU. We handle feasibility, design, permits, and construction.

How long will my Compton remodel or addition take?

It depends on scope — bathrooms typically run 3–5 weeks, kitchens 4–8 weeks, and room additions 2–4 months. We give you a firm schedule with milestones in your written estimate.
