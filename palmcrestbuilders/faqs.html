Remodeling & Construction FAQs

Everything you wanted to know about building and remodeling.

Do you handle design and permits?

Yes. We manage design, drawings, and permit coordination with your local building department as part of our design-build and project management service, then coordinate licensed trade contractors to execute the build — so you get one team, one plan, and one point of contact from start to finish.

How much does a remodel or addition cost in LA?

It depends heavily on scope and finishes. As rough LA-market guidance: bathroom remodels often start in the low-to-mid five figures, kitchens in the mid five figures and up, room additions and ADUs commonly run into the low six figures, and custom homes are priced per project. Every estimate we provide is written and itemized so you see exactly where your budget goes.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. Remodels, additions, ADUs, and structural work require permits through your city or LA County building department. We prepare drawings, submit for plan check, pull the permits, and schedule every required inspection so your project is legal and documented.

Can I build an ADU on my property?

Most single-family lots in LA County now qualify for at least one ADU, and often a Junior ADU as well. We evaluate your lot, confirm the size and type allowed, and handle feasibility, design, permits, and construction. Learn more about our ADU services.

What is design-build, and why does it matter?

Design-build means one accountable team manages both the design and the construction under a single contract. You avoid the gaps and finger-pointing that happen when architects, subs, and suppliers work in silos — and you get one accountable team, one schedule, and one point of contact.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes — we're fully insured and bonded, and our California Class B contractor license application is in progress with the CSLB. We provide design and project management and work alongside licensed trade contractors for execution. You can verify any California license at cslb.ca.gov.

How long will my project take?

Bathrooms typically run 3–5 weeks, kitchens 4–8 weeks, room additions 2–4 months, ADUs 3–8 months, and custom homes 10–18 months. Permitting adds time up front. We give you a firm milestone schedule in your written estimate.

Can we live in our home during the remodel?

For phased or single-room projects, usually yes. For full-gut or major structural work we often recommend relocating for safety and speed. We'll give you an honest recommendation based on your specific scope.

What areas do you serve?

All of Los Angeles County — the San Fernando Valley, Santa Clarita, San Gabriel Valley, Westside, South Bay, Long Beach, and beyond — plus surrounding Southern California. See our full service area list.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes — free, no-obligation project consultations with a written estimate. We'll walk your space, talk through options and budget, and give you a clear plan with no sales pressure. Request a free consultation.
