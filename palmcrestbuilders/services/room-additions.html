Our Process

Step 1: Free Consultation

We review your goals, walk the property, and discuss feasibility, setbacks, and budget.

Step 2: Design & Written Estimate

We prepare plans and structural engineering, then deliver an itemized estimate and schedule.

Step 3: Permits & Planning

We submit for plan check and pull all permits through your city or LA County building department.

Step 4: Construction

Foundation, framing, mechanicals, roofing tie-in, insulation, and finishes — built to match your home.

Step 5: Final Walk-Through & Warranty

We complete final inspections, walk the addition with you, and provide full warranty documentation.

First-Floor vs. Second-Story Additions

A ground-floor addition is usually simpler and less expensive, but it uses up yard space and requires available lot area within your setbacks. A second-story addition preserves your lot but adds cost for structural reinforcement, a new staircase, and temporary weather protection. During your consultation we'll evaluate your lot, foundation, and goals and recommend the approach that gives you the most usable space for your budget.

Room Additions FAQs

How long does a room addition take?

A single-room, first-floor addition typically takes 2–4 months. Larger suites and second-story additions run 4–7 months including permitting.

Will the addition match my existing home?

Yes — matching rooflines, stucco or siding, windows, and interior finishes is a core part of our work so the addition looks original.

Do additions require structural engineering?

Almost always. We include engineering and Title 24 energy calculations, and handle plan check and permits as part of the project.
