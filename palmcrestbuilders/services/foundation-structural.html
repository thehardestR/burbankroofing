Seismic Retrofitting in Southern California

Older homes across Los Angeles — especially those built before modern codes — often have cripple walls or soft-story conditions that perform poorly in an earthquake. A seismic retrofit bolts your home to its foundation, braces cripple walls, and reinforces weak points. It's one of the highest-value structural upgrades a Southern California homeowner can make, and we handle the engineering, permits, and construction as one package.

Foundation & Structural FAQs

How do I know if I have foundation problems?

Common signs include sloping floors, sticking doors and windows, stair-step cracks in stucco or drywall, and gaps at baseboards. We'll assess and give you an honest evaluation.

Do you do earthquake retrofitting?

Yes. We perform cripple-wall and soft-story seismic retrofits with engineered hardware, permits, and inspections.

Is structural work permitted and engineered?

Always. Structural and foundation work requires engineering and permits — we manage both so the work is safe, legal, and documented.
