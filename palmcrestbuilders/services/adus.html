Our Process

Why Build an ADU?

California has made ADUs dramatically easier to build — most single-family lots now qualify for at least one ADU and often a Junior ADU as well. Homeowners use them for rental income, multigenerational living, home offices, and long-term property value. Because ADU rules change and vary by city, we stay current on state law and local ordinances so your project is designed to approve the first time and built to code.

ADUs (Accessory Dwelling Units) FAQs

Can I build an ADU on my property?

Most single-family and many multifamily lots in LA County qualify. During your consultation we confirm the ADU type and maximum size your specific lot allows.

How long does an ADU take to build?

Garage conversions often take 3–4 months; detached new-construction ADUs typically run 5–8 months including permitting.

How big can my ADU be?

Detached ADUs are commonly allowed up to 1,200 sq ft, and conversions can match the existing structure. Local rules vary — we'll confirm your limits.
