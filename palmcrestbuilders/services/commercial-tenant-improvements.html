Our Process

Step 1: Free Consultation

We review your space, lease requirements, and target opening date, and discuss scope and budget.

Step 2: Design & Written Estimate

We coordinate plans with your architect, then deliver an itemized estimate and construction schedule.

Step 3: Permits & Approvals

We manage building department plan check, permits, and any landlord or HOA approvals.

Step 4: Construction

Demo, framing, MEP, finishes, and signage coordination — sequenced to hit your opening date.

Step 5: Inspection & Turnover

We complete inspections, secure sign-offs, and turn over a business-ready space.

Built Around Your Opening Date

In commercial work the schedule is the project. Every week of delay is a week of rent without revenue, so we plan procurement, inspections, and trade sequencing to protect your opening date. We coordinate closely with your architect, landlord, and local building and health departments so approvals don't become bottlenecks — and you open on time.

Commercial Tenant Improvements FAQs

Do you work with our architect and landlord?

Yes. We coordinate directly with your design team, landlord, and property manager, and manage all required approvals and permits.

Can you handle ADA and code upgrades?

Yes. Tenant improvements often trigger ADA and code upgrades. We identify them early and build compliance into the scope and budget.

How fast can our build-out be completed?

Simple office TIs can finish in 4–8 weeks; restaurants and medical suites take longer due to MEP and health-department requirements. We give you a firm schedule up front.
