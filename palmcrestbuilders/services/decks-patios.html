Wood vs. Composite Decking

Natural wood is beautiful and cost-effective up front but needs regular sealing and maintenance in the Southern California sun. Composite and PVC decking cost more initially but resist fading, splintering, and rot, and need almost no maintenance. During your consultation we'll compare materials, lifespan, and cost so you get the outdoor space that fits your budget and how much upkeep you want to do.

Decks & Patios FAQs

Do I need a permit for a deck or patio cover?

Ground-level patios often don't, but elevated decks, attached covers, and outdoor kitchens usually do. We handle any permits your project needs.

How long does a deck or patio take to build?

Most decks and patio covers take 1–3 weeks depending on size, elevation, and whether footings and permits are required.

Can you build on a hillside or sloped yard?

Yes. Elevated and hillside decks are a specialty — we engineer proper footings and structural connections for safety.
